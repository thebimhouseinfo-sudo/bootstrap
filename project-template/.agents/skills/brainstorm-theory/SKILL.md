# brainstorm-theory

## Procedure
1. Planner (not Advisor) clarifies outcome and simplest workable demonstration; interpret platform examples as analogies unless confirmed.
2. Clarify personal/team/commercial/market use, current and actually committed future scale, trust/data access, real safety/reliability needs and explicit non-goals.
3. Propose only proportionate alternatives needed to resolve open decisions; compare observable outcomes and limitations rather than making human read technical plans.
4. Record initial proposed theory as OPEN in the single CRITERIA draft. For later changes to approved theory, discuss a proposed delta **without editing CRITERIA** until the user has settled it and asked to apply.
5. Invoke Advisor on the **full initial draft** before first approval, or on a **settled amendment** only when the user asks to apply, before writing it; then obtain human approval. Do not infer consent.

## Output
Clear user-facing results/exclusions/tradeoffs, evidence, OPEN questions and next authorized decision. No parallel theory source or fabricated approval.
